  Phase 7 - Premium quotation, Designation Master, Factory Profile                        DONE
    - Quotation icon in the admin header: pick client and services, the premium one-page A4
      quotation (brand header, pillar icons, itemised table, GST summary, inclusions, notes,
      payment terms, acceptance block) opens as a PDF. Defaults come from Settings > Billing.
    - Designation Master: 1,001 designations in 10 industries loaded from the client's document
      (`npm run db:seed-designations`). Full add / edit / delete for admin (shared master) and
      employer (own rows); the employee form picks from it and prefills department, skill grade
      and wages. A designation in use cannot be deleted; renaming follows through to employees.
    - Factory Profile: one per employer (licences, people, units, departments, holidays, land,
      welfare, treatment plants, power / boiler). Employer edits its own; admin opens any company
      from the employer page.
    - Fixed on the way: the submission PDF used wrong employer column names (500 error), the
      browser tab had no favicon link, and the Factory Profile menu icon.
    - Tests: verify-designations-factory 63 checks, browser audit 30 checks (desktop and phone).
    - Note: the document's section totals (e.g. 150) do not match the names listed; 527 skill
      levels were inferred by keywords and are flagged "level_inferred".
  Phase 8 - CAPAR per the client's "Act and CAPAR" document                                DONE
    - Act library: Act > Chapter > Section > Sub Section, each with Explain text and Amendments
      (admin: Compliance > Acts > "Chapters & sections"). The auditor searches it inside the CAPAR row and
      the path, requirement and amendments fill in (still editable; typing the Act by hand also works).
    - CAPAR row (item form): status Incomplete / Not findings / Verified (Verified needs finding, root cause,
      action plan and timeline, and records who signed off and when), Compliance + evidence images,
      Recommendations, Verification / Assessment. The verifier's Eligible / Not Eligible is unchanged.
      Existing rows were moved to the new status. The PDF prints the new fields.
    - Audit Documents (the client's "Factory Profile" checklist): 258 documents in 8 sections
      (Factory Profile, Recruitment, Wages, Employee Treatment, Health & Safety, Management Systems, SA8000, BSCI).
      The employer lists documents (Provided / Not applicable, reference, validity, notes, files) and submits;
      every submission is a dated snapshot. Admin edits the checklist and chooses which sections each auditor gets.
    - Gate: an auditor with sections must download the employer's latest submitted pack (PDF) before a CAPAR can
      be started for that company (409 with the reason). Downloads are recorded. Admin is never held up; an
      auditor with no sections has nothing to download.
    - Tests: verify-capar-pack 76 checks, browser audit 60 checks; verify-capar 96 still passes.
    - Not built yet: the "Audit / Assessment Structure" table (the document gives no columns), the Garment
      checklist and the Legal Register part of the same document (next topic).
    - Known: the document-pack checklist is built from the clean first part of the Word file (it repeats and
      mixes other lists further down). Health & Safety shows 68 items against 67 numbered in the file.
